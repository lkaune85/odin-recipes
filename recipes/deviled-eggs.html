Deviled Eggs

Home

Description

This recipe contains the secret sauce to making easy and quick deviled eggs.

Ingredients

  • 12 Hard Boild Eggs
  • 1/3 cup Mayo
  • 2 Tablespoons pickle juice
  • 1 1/2 teaspoon dijon mustard
  • Salt and Pepper
  • Paprika

Steps

  1. Peel and halve hard boiled eggs.
  2. Mix egg yolks, mayo, pickle juice and mustard.
  3. Add salt and pepper to desired taste.
  4. Divide mixture up between egg halves.
  5. Add small amount of paprika on each egg.
  6. Lasts up to 3 days.
