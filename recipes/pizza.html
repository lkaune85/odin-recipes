Pizza Dough

Home

Description

Pizza dough recipes are always too hard! Let's simplify it.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of flour
  • 1 packet of instant yeast
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons sugar
  • 3/4 salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 2 Tablespoons olive oil
  • 3/4 cup warm water

Steps

  1. Mix 1 cup of flour, yeast, sugar, salt, and garlic powder.
  2. Stir in olive oil and warm water.
  3. Slowly add rest of flour until the dough forms a ball (should be sticky).
  4. Brush another bowl with olive oil.
  5. Put a small amount of flour on both hands and for the dough into a ball. Transfer to the oiled bowl and roll around until covered.
  6. Set aside for 30mins (dough should double in size).
  7. Preheat oven to 425F.
  8. Cover a surface in flour and transfer dough to surface. Knead until smooth.
  9. Roll out dough into one large pizza (or multiple small ones).
  10. Place dough on pizza stone.
  11. Cover in desired sauce and toppings.
  12. Bake for 14 mins.
